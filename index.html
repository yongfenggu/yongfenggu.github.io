Publications

NEURIPS 2026 PAPER
Harnessing Agentic Evolution
Jiayi Zhang, Yongfeng Gu, Jianhao Ruan, Maojia Song, Yiran Peng, Zhiguang Han, Jinyu Xiang,..., Bang Liu, Chenglin Wu, Yuyu Luo
Reframes agentic evolution as an interactive environment: instead of generating solutions directly, a meta-agent edits the workflow / task-agent that controls later evolution, giving a unified interface for both workflow- and agent-based evolution over long horizons. It outperforms strong baselines on ARC-AGI-2 and Terminal-Bench.
ARXIV 2026 PAPER
Foundation Protocol: A Coordination Layer for Agentic Society
Bang Liu, Yongfeng Gu, Jiayi Zhang, Zhaoyang Yu, Sirui Hong, Maojia Song, Xiaoqiang Wang,..., Qiang Yang, Yuyu Luo, Chenglin Wu
A graph-native coordination layer for the AI society. It models heterogeneous entities — agents, tools, resources, people, and institutions — as addressable nodes, and organizes coordination across a four-plane architecture: Entity & Trust, Transport & Routing, Interaction & Organization, and Regulation & Oversight.
